WILDLIFE CONSERVATION

Meet PNG’s Most Incredible Animals & Flora

Papua New Guinea is home to 5% of global biodiversity. At the Nature Park, get up close with rare endemic species found nowhere else on earth.

Endangered
Dendrolagus goodfellowi

Goodfellow’s Tree Kangaroo

One of PNG’s most treasured tree-dwelling marsupials. Our park is an international leader in breeding and rehabilitation research.

PNG National Icon
Paradisaea raggiana

Raggiana Bird of Paradise

Featured on the National Flag of Papua New Guinea. Experience their courtship displays and vibrant plumage in our walk-through aviary.

Keystone Rainforest Bird
Casuarius casuarius

Southern Cassowary

The ancient dinosaur of modern birds. Known as the gardener of the rainforest for seed dispersal across the vast highlands and lowlands.

Vulnerable
Goura victoria

Victoria Crowned Pigeon

The world’s largest pigeon species. Distinguished by an exquisite lace-like blue crest and deep rhythmic cooing echoing through the park.

3,000+ PNG Species
Orchidaceae Papua

National Orchid Collection

Papua New Guinea holds 60% of the world's orchid diversity. Stroll through thousands of blooming wild and cultivated specimens.

Native Reptile
Morelia viridis

Papuan Green Tree Python

Fascinating non-venomous tree serpent native to PNG rainforests. Hatchlings are bright lemon yellow before transforming into brilliant emerald.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about tickets, cameras, feeding, and directions.

What are the general admission prices for PNG residents?

For PNG residents, tickets are K15.00 for Adults, K10.00 for Children (ages 3–12), and K12.00 for Students with a valid student ID. Infants under 3 years enter completely free of charge!

Are visitors allowed to feed the animals?

For the health and safety of our native species, feeding animals outside of supervised keeper programs is strictly prohibited. You can join our scheduled keeper feeding sessions throughout the day where keepers share nutritious, biologically appropriate diets with the cassowaries and tree kangaroos.

How do I get to Port Moresby Nature Park?

We are situated on Goro Kaeaga Road in Waigani, right between the University of Papua New Guinea (UPNG) and Port Moresby National High School. Ample free, secure on-site parking is available.

How do schools book an excursion or guided tour?

Schools can easily submit an excursion request through our online booking modal on this website, or call our Education Department directly at +675 7230 1805. We recommend booking at least 1–2 weeks in advance.
